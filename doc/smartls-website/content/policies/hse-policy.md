## Our Commitment

Freight is heavy, and the work around it is dangerous: a container lifted on a quay, a transformer chained to a low-loader, a truck on the road to N'Djamena at night. No shipment is worth an injury. We plan every movement so that the people who carry it out go home safe, and we take responsibility for the effect our work has on the places it passes through.

This policy applies to our staff in Douala and Mannheim, to the warehouses and vehicles we operate, and to the subcontractors we engage to move, store or handle cargo on our behalf.

## What We Commit To

- **Stop the job when it is unsafe.** Anyone working on our behalf may stop an operation they believe to be unsafe, and will not be blamed for doing so.
- **Plan heavy and out-of-gauge cargo before it moves.** Lifting plans, lashing and securing, route surveys, escorts and permits are settled in writing before an abnormal load leaves the port.
- **Safe roads.** We require the hauliers we engage to use roadworthy vehicles, rested drivers and loads secured to the standard of the cargo. We plan long inland journeys with rest stops and avoid night driving where the route makes it hazardous.
- **Dangerous goods handled by the rules.** Hazardous cargo is accepted only with correct classification, documents, packaging and labels, under the IMDG Code at sea, IATA rules in the air and ADR on European roads, and is stored apart from other goods.
- **Protective equipment.** Staff and subcontractors on quays, in warehouses and at loading sites wear the protective equipment the task requires.
- **Less waste, less fuel.** We consolidate cargo to fill containers and trucks, plan routes to avoid empty running, and choose rail to Ngaoundéré where it serves the shipment as well as the road.
- **No harm left behind.** Spills, damaged packaging and waste from our operations are cleaned up and disposed of lawfully.

## Incidents

Every accident, injury, near miss, spill and damage is reported the same day, recorded and investigated. We look for the cause and change how we work. Serious incidents are reported to the Managing Director at once and to the authorities where the law requires it.

## Subcontractors

We choose hauliers, warehouses and handling contractors who can show they work safely, we brief them on the risks of each job, and we stop using a contractor who puts people or the environment at risk.

## Review

Our Operational Excellence Committee reviews incidents and near misses every quarter, with the changes made in response. The Managing Director reviews this policy every year and is accountable for it. Every person working for us is responsible for their own safety and for that of the people around them.
