## Our Commitment

A client who gives us a shipment is trusting us with three things: that it will arrive when we said, that it will cost what we said, and that every document it needs will be in order. Quality, for us, is keeping those three promises, file after file, and being honest early when one of them is at risk.

This policy applies to every shipment handled by Smart Logistics and Services, in Douala, in Mannheim and through the partners we appoint at the far end.

## What We Commit To

- **A named file owner for every shipment.** One person knows its documents, its costs and its next step, and the client knows who that person is.
- **The full price, in writing.** Our proposals state port charges, duties, taxes and inland costs. What we quote is what the client pays, unless the shipment changes.
- **Customs first.** Classification, certificates of origin, exemptions and permits are settled before the cargo moves, because a file that is clean at the border is faster and cheaper than one repaired there.
- **News before it is asked for.** The client hears about a delay, a hold or a change of plan from us, with the reason and what we are doing about it, before they have to chase.
- **Partners we answer for.** Where the other end of a shipment is not our own office, we choose the agent, brief them in writing and remain the client's single contact until delivery.
- **Records that can be checked.** Every instruction, document and decision on a file is kept in our operating system, so that what happened can be shown, not remembered.

## How We Keep It

**On every file.** The file owner checks the documents against the shipment before release, confirms the route and the cost with the client in writing, and records each milestone as it happens.

**Every week.** Operations reviews open files: anything late, held or over budget is named, with an owner and a date.

**Every quarter.** Our Operational Excellence Committee reviews how we performed: shipments delivered on the date promised, customs holds and their causes, claims, complaints and the time it took to resolve them. Each review ends with changes to how we work, and the next review checks that they were made.

**Every year.** Our Strategic Planning Committee sets the objectives this policy is measured against, and the Chief Executive Officer reviews this policy and publishes any change.

## When Something Goes Wrong

We record every complaint, claim and non-conformity, whoever caused it. We tell the client what happened and what we are doing about it. We find the cause, not only the person, and we change the process so that it does not happen again. A client complaint is answered by a named person within one working day.

## Suppliers and Partners

Carriers, hauliers, warehouses and overseas agents are chosen for their record, not only their price. We review the ones we use regularly, and we stop using a partner whose service puts our promises to clients at risk.

## Responsibility

The Chief Executive Officer is accountable for this policy. Every member of staff is responsible for applying it to the files they handle, and for raising a risk to a client's shipment the moment they see it.
