## Who This Applies To

Every member of staff in Douala and Mannheim, whatever their role or seniority, and everyone who acts in our name: agents, subcontractors and consultants. When we appoint a partner, we expect them to work to this Code on our shipments.

## Our Word

We say what we will do, we put it in writing, and we do it. When we cannot, we say so early and plainly. We never tell a client a shipment has moved when it has not, never alter a document to make a file look cleaner than it is, and never quote a price we know to be incomplete.

## With Clients

- Treat every client's cargo, documents and data as if they were our own, and use their information only for the shipment they gave us.
- Give clients the full picture, including what could go wrong and what it would cost.
- Keep client information confidential, inside and outside the company, during and after employment with us.

## With Authorities

- Declare goods truthfully: description, value, origin and tariff classification as they are, not as they would be cheaper.
- Pay duties, taxes and fees in full, through official channels, against official receipts.
- Offer nothing to an official to obtain, speed up or avoid any decision (see our Anti-Corruption Policy).
- Respect sanctions and export controls. We do not move goods for, or to, people and places that the law forbids.

## With Partners and Suppliers

Choose carriers, hauliers, warehouses and agents on their record, their price and their integrity. Pay them what we agreed, on time. Accept no gift, payment or favour that could influence the choice.

## With One Another

- Treat colleagues with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.
- Work safely and stop any job that is unsafe (see our Health, Safety and Environment Policy).
- Declare any conflict of interest: a family member at a supplier, a share in a competitor, a second job that touches our business.

## Company Property and Records

Use company money, vehicles, systems and information for company work. Keep records accurate and complete, in the system where they belong. Never destroy or hide a record that an audit, a claim or an investigation may need.

## Speaking Up

Anyone who sees a breach of this Code, or is asked to commit one, should say so: to their manager, or directly and in confidence to the Chief Executive Officer. We protect anyone who raises a concern in good faith, and we do not tolerate retaliation against them.

## Consequences

A breach of this Code is treated as a disciplinary matter and may lead to dismissal. A breach by a partner may end our work with them. Where the law has been broken, we report it to the authorities.

## Responsibility

The Chief Executive Officer is accountable for this Code and reviews it every year. Managers make sure their teams know it. Every person who works for us is responsible for following it.
