## Why It Matters to a Forwarder

Trade between Europe and Central Africa is being asked new questions. European buyers want to know where a sack of cocoa or a log of timber was grown before they accept it. Carriers now charge for the carbon their ships emit. Development agencies and project owners ask how their contractors treat people. A forwarder that cannot answer for its own conduct cannot help its clients answer for theirs. This policy sets out how we answer.

## Environment

- **Fill what moves.** We consolidate cargo so containers and trucks travel full, and plan inland routes to avoid empty return legs.
- **Choose the lower-carbon route where it serves the shipment.** Rail from Douala to Ngaoundéré before road where transit time and cost allow; sea before air unless the client needs speed.
- **Carry the documents the rules require.** We help exporters assemble the traceability and origin evidence European rules increasingly demand, and we do not knowingly handle goods whose origin is illegal.
- **Count what we can.** We record the distance, mode and weight of the shipments we arrange, so that we can report their emissions to clients who need the figure.
- **Waste.** Packaging, dunnage and waste from our operations are reused where possible and disposed of lawfully.

## Social

- **Our people.** Fair pay, paid on time; written contracts; safe working conditions (see our Health, Safety and Environment Policy); and promotion on merit.
- **No forced or child labour,** in our business or among the hauliers, warehouses and agents we engage.
- **Equal treatment,** whatever a person's sex, origin, religion or background, in hiring, pay and promotion.
- **The communities we pass through.** Our drivers and subcontractors respect local people and roads, and we answer complaints from the communities along our routes.
- **Humanitarian cargo.** When we move relief and development supplies, we treat lead times as commitments to the people waiting for them.

## Governance

- **Two committees, one table.** Our Strategic Planning Committee sets the company's direction and objectives. Our Operational Excellence Committee reviews how we performed, what went wrong and what we changed, with compliance and risk considered at the same table.
- **Integrity.** We do not bribe and do not tolerate bribery (see our Anti-Corruption Policy), and every member of staff is bound by our Code of Conduct.
- **Records.** Our operations, accounts and documents are kept in one system, so that what we report can be checked.
- **Data.** We protect the personal and commercial data clients entrust to us (see our Privacy Policy).

## Review

The Chief Executive Officer is accountable for this policy and reviews it every year with the Strategic Planning Committee. Progress against it is part of the Operational Excellence Committee's quarterly review.
