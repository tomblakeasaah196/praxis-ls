## Our Position

Smart Logistics and Services does not offer, promise, give, request or accept a bribe, in any form, to or from anyone, anywhere. That includes small payments to speed up a routine step, often called facilitation payments. A shipment that can only move by paying a bribe does not move.

We hold this line because our clients, many of them international organisations and project owners, must be able to show where every franc and every euro of their logistics budget went. And because a forwarder that pays its way through a border once is expected to pay every time.

This policy applies to all staff in Douala and Mannheim, and to every agent, haulier, broker and consultant acting for us. It is written to comply with the laws of Cameroon and Germany, and with the anti-corruption laws that apply to our clients.

## What Is Not Allowed

- Giving or offering money, goods, services, employment or any advantage to a customs officer, port official, police or gendarmerie officer, or any other public official, to obtain, speed up or avoid a decision.
- Paying an unofficial "fee" at a port, a checkpoint or a border, whatever it is called and however small.
- Giving anything of value to an employee of a client, supplier or partner to win or keep business.
- Accepting anything of value that could influence how we choose a supplier or treat a client.
- Using an agent, haulier or any third party to do what we may not do ourselves.

## Official Fees Are Different

Duties, taxes, port charges, inspection fees and other charges set by law are paid in full, to the authority entitled to them, against an official receipt that goes on the file. If a charge cannot be supported by an official receipt or a published tariff, it is not paid until the Managing Director has reviewed it.

## Safety Comes First

If a member of staff or a driver is threatened with harm unless they pay, they may pay to protect themselves. They must report it to the Managing Director the same day, and the payment is recorded as what it was.

## Gifts and Hospitality

Modest gifts and hospitality that are customary, given openly and could not be mistaken for an inducement are allowed: a meal during a business visit, a diary at the year's end. Anything of greater value, anything offered to or by a public official, and anything offered while a decision is pending must be declared to the Managing Director before it is given or accepted, and is recorded.

## Agents and Partners

We choose our agents and subcontractors with care, tell them in writing that they must not pay bribes on our behalf, and pay them only against invoices for real services at fair prices, into accounts in their own name. We end the relationship with any partner who breaks this rule.

## Records

Every payment we make is recorded accurately, with its purpose and its receipt, in our accounting system. No payment is disguised, split or left off the books.

## Speaking Up

Anyone who is asked for a bribe, or suspects one, should report it to the Managing Director, in person or in writing, in confidence. No one will be penalised for refusing to pay a bribe, even if the refusal costs us a shipment or a client. Retaliation against a person who reports in good faith is itself a breach of this policy.

## Consequences

Breaching this policy is gross misconduct and may lead to dismissal and to referral to the authorities. A breach by an agent or partner ends our work with them.

## Responsibility

The Managing Director is accountable for this policy and reviews it every year. The Operational Excellence Committee reviews every reported request, gift declaration and unreceipted charge at its quarterly meeting.
