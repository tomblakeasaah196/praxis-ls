**Meta title.** Customs Brokerage in Douala | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Customs clearance in Douala and Kribi: import and export declarations, tariff classification, duties and taxes, exemptions and release.

### What we do
We prepare and file your import and export declarations, classify the goods, check the values and
origin, calculate the duties and taxes, and see the cargo through inspection to release. We read the
file before customs does: an invoice that does not match the packing list is corrected before it is
filed, not after it is stopped.

### Where
The ports of Douala and Kribi and Douala International Airport.

### Documents
The commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or air waybill, certificate of origin, the
cargo tracking note (BESC), and any licence, exemption or certificate the goods need. We tell you at
the outset which ones your shipment requires.

### What you receive
A list of the documents your goods need before they arrive. The customs assessment before anything
is paid. The receipts for every duty and tax. The release, and a clearance file you can show your
auditors.

**Highlights.** Import and export declarations · Tariff classification and valuation · Duties, taxes and exemptions · Inspection and release · Douala, Kribi and the airport · A clearance file for your auditors

**Meta title.** Business Representation in Cameroon | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Local representation for foreign companies and project contractors in Cameroon: customs, ports, administrations and suppliers, under your written mandate.

### What we do
We act for foreign companies, project contractors and organisations that work in Cameroon without
an office of their own. Under your written mandate we deal with customs, the ports and the
administrations, follow your import files, meet suppliers and report back. You decide; we are your
presence at the counter.

### Where
Douala, Yaoundé and the ports of Douala and Kribi.

### Documents
Your mandate and power of attorney, then the documents each task calls for. We keep copies of
everything we file in your name.

### What you receive
A named contact, a written report for every task, and copies of every document we filed or received
on your behalf.

**Highlights.** Under your written mandate · Customs, ports and administrations · Supplier visits and follow-up · A named contact · A written report for every task
