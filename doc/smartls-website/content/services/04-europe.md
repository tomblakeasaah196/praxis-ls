(guide section 10): we organise and coordinate; partners haul, store and declare. We never say we own
trucks or a warehouse in Europe, file EU customs declarations ourselves, or buy goods on our own
account.

## Export Consolidation to Africa

Featured: yes. Accent: primary. Cover: `12-europe-consolidation.jpg`.

**Homepage line.** Goods from several European suppliers, gathered and shipped to Douala as one consignment.

**Closing line.** One booking, one bill of lading, however many suppliers you buy from.

**Meta title.** Consolidation from Europe to Cameroon | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Buy from several European suppliers and ship once: collections organised across Europe, consolidation with partner warehouses, and sea freight to Douala and Kribi.

### What we do
We organise the collection from each of your European suppliers with partner hauliers, have every
delivery checked against its order at a partner consolidation warehouse, and book the combined
consignment, a full container or groupage, for Douala or Kribi. A licensed partner files the export
declaration. Our team in Douala takes it from the berth.

### Where
Suppliers across Germany and the European Union, coordinated from our office in Mannheim, sailing
from Antwerp, Rotterdam or Hamburg.

### Documents
Each supplier's invoice and packing list, a consolidated packing list, the export declaration filed
by our partner, the bill of lading, and the certificate of origin and BESC for arrival in Cameroon.

### What you receive
A receipt for each supplier's delivery, with photographs of anything damaged. One consignment, one
bill of lading, one file reference followed through to delivery in Cameroon.

**Highlights.** Several suppliers, one shipment · Each delivery checked against its order · Collections and consolidation organised from Mannheim · Antwerp, Rotterdam and Hamburg · Followed through to Douala

## Sourcing and Supplier Coordination

Featured: no. Accent: accent. Cover: `14-europe-sourcing.jpg`.

**Homepage line.** Finding and following your European suppliers, so the order that ships is the one you placed.

**Closing line.** You buy from the supplier; we make sure the goods and the papers are right.

**Meta title.** Supplier Coordination in Europe for African Importers | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** For importers in Cameroon and Central Africa: supplier search, quotes, order follow-up, readiness checks and shipping documents, coordinated from Mannheim.

### What we do
For buyers in Cameroon and Central Africa, we identify European suppliers, ask them for quotes,
follow your order through production, and confirm the goods are ready and packed before collection.
You contract and pay the supplier directly; we do not buy on our own account. We make sure the
invoice and documents are the ones customs in Douala will ask for.

### Where
From our office in Mannheim, across Germany and the European Union.

### Documents
The supplier's proforma and final invoice, the packing list, any conformity or origin certificates,
and our readiness report.

### What you receive
Comparable supplier quotes, an order followed to the factory gate, a readiness report with
photographs, and documents ready for export and for clearance at home.

**Highlights.** Supplier search and quotes · Order follow-up · Readiness checks with photographs · Documents ready for Douala customs · You keep the contract with your supplier

## EU Import Clearance

Featured: no. Accent: accent. Cover: `11b-service-business-representation-alt.jpg`.

**Homepage line.** Bringing African goods into the European Union, cleared by licensed partner brokers.

**Closing line.** Cocoa, coffee and timber arrive with the papers Europe now asks for.

**Meta title.** EU Import Clearance for African Exports | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Customs clearance into the European Union for exports from Cameroon and Central Africa: files prepared with us, declarations filed by licensed partner brokers.

### What we do
For goods shipped from Cameroon and Central Africa into Europe, we advise on what the goods need,
prepare the file in Douala, and arrange the EU import declaration with a licensed partner broker, who
files it. The origin, the certificates and the data Europe requires are ready before the cargo
sails.

### Where
The main ports of entry in Northern Europe, coordinated from our Mannheim office.

### Documents
The commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, phytosanitary
certificate where the goods need one, the importer's EORI number, and the due diligence statement
the EU deforestation rules require for products such as cocoa, coffee and wood when they apply.

### What you receive
A checklist for your goods before they leave Africa, the import declaration filed by our partner
broker, the duties and import VAT assessment, and the release.

**Highlights.** Import into the EU · Declarations filed by licensed partner brokers · Documents checked before sailing · Coordinated from Mannheim
