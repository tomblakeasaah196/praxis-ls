**Meta title.** Sea Freight to Douala and Kribi | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Sea freight into and out of Cameroon and Central Africa: FCL and LCL through Douala and Kribi, export and import clearance, port release and delivery inland.

### What we do
We book your containers or groupage space with the carrier, prepare the shipping documents, and
follow the cargo from the port of loading to the berth. At destination we clear it, settle the port
and carrier charges, take the delivery order and bring it to you. Exporting, we collect from your
premises, clear the goods for export and see them loaded. You tell us where our work begins and
ends, and the quote covers exactly that.

### Where
Douala, the country's main port, and Kribi, the deep-water port to the south, which takes the larger
vessels. From Europe we route through Antwerp, Rotterdam, Hamburg and Le Havre, with our Mannheim
office at the European end. From Asia and the Americas, through our partner network at the port of
loading. Inland, cargo continues to Yaoundé, the north of Cameroon, N'Djamena and Bangui.

### Documents
The commercial invoice and packing list, the bill of lading, the certificate of origin where the
goods claim one, and the cargo tracking note Cameroon requires for every shipment (the BESC). At
import, the customs declaration and the payment of duties. At export, the export declaration and any
licence or phytosanitary certificate the goods need.

### What you receive
A written quote that names the Incoterm and every leg it covers. The booking confirmation, the
vessel and its estimated arrival. A file reference you follow online from booking to delivery. On
arrival, the customs assessment before anything is paid. At the end, a signed proof of delivery and
one invoice.

**Highlights.** FCL and LCL · Douala and Kribi · Export and import clearance · Port release and delivery order · Delivery inland and to landlocked destinations · One file reference from booking to delivery

`CONFIRM`: reefer containers and dangerous goods. If we take them, add "Reefer and dangerous goods on request".

**Meta title.** Air Freight to and from Cameroon | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Air freight through Douala and Yaoundé Nsimalen: airline booking, screening, the air waybill, export and import clearance and delivery to the door.

### What we do
We book space with the airline or a consolidator, confirm the flight and the cut-off in writing, and
deliver the cargo screened and documented to the airline. At destination we clear it and deliver it.
For a spare part that has stopped a production line, we look for the earliest flight first and the
lowest rate second, and we tell you which one we chose.

### Where
Douala International and Yaoundé Nsimalen in Cameroon. In Europe, through Frankfurt, Paris Charles de
Gaulle and Brussels, a short drive from our Mannheim office. Elsewhere, through our partner network
at the airport of departure.

### Documents
The air waybill, which is the contract of carriage. The commercial invoice and packing list, and the
certificate of origin where it is needed. The security declaration every air shipment carries. At
import, the customs declaration; at export, the export declaration and any licence the goods need.

### What you receive
The flight, the cut-off and the rate in writing before you book. The air waybill number on the day
of departure. Arrival notice, customs assessment and delivery, all on one file reference. Proof of
delivery and one invoice.

**Highlights.** Airline booking and confirmed cut-offs · Screening and the security declaration · Air waybill issued for you · Export and import clearance · Douala and Yaoundé Nsimalen · Delivery to the door

`CONFIRM`: dangerous goods and perishables by air.

**Meta title.** Project Cargo and Break-bulk | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Project and break-bulk shipping into Cameroon, Chad and the CAR: route surveys, port handling at Douala and Kribi, permits, escorts and delivery to site.

### What we do
We move what does not fit a container: transformers, generators, steel structures, vehicles and
plant. We start from the drawings and the lifting points, survey the road from the port to the site,
and agree the discharge with the terminal before the vessel sails. Then we clear the cargo, arrange
the permits and escorts for abnormal loads, and deliver it to the foundations it was built for.

### Where
Discharge at Douala or Kribi, chosen by the vessel, the weight and the road beyond. Delivery anywhere
in Cameroon, and onward to Chad and the Central African Republic. For projects that start in Europe,
our Mannheim office works with the supplier at the factory gate.

### Documents
The packing list with dimensions, weights and centre of gravity, the lifting and stowage plans, the
bill of lading, the commercial invoice and the certificate of origin. For the road, the abnormal load
permits. Where goods cross into a landlocked country, the transit declaration and its guarantee.

### What you receive
A route survey and a movement plan before you commit. One coordinator from the factory to the site,
a daily position while the cargo moves, and photographs at each handover. At the end, the signed
delivery to site and one invoice.

**Highlights.** Out-of-gauge and heavy lifts · Route surveys before booking · Douala and Kribi · Permits and escorts · Transit to Chad and the CAR · One coordinator from factory to site
