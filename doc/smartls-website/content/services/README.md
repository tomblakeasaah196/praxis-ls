## How a quote routes (Website tab › Quoting)

Sea Freight quotes for Sea Freight Import, Sea Freight Export and End-to-End Sea Freight; Air Freight
for its import, export and end-to-end types. The generic routing decides between them from the work
the visitor describes: door to door goes to End to End, otherwise to the import or export type. Every
other service quotes for its own type only.
