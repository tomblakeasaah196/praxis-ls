**Homepage line.** From the Douala quay to N'Djamena and Bangui, in transit and under bond.

**Closing line.** Your cargo crosses Cameroon in transit, so its duties are paid where it arrives.

**Meta title.** Transit to Chad and the CAR from Douala | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Hinterland transit from Douala and Kribi to N'Djamena and Bangui: rail to Ngaoundéré, road beyond, transit declarations, guarantees and border handover.

### What we do
We take cargo for Chad and the Central African Republic from the quay to the consignee, across
Cameroon in transit, so it is not cleared or taxed in Cameroon. We lodge the transit declaration,
put up the guarantee, choose rail or road for each leg, and hand the cargo over at the border and at
destination. The reverse runs too: exports from Chad and the CAR out through Douala.

### Where
To N'Djamena: by rail from Douala to Ngaoundéré, where the line ends, then by road north through
Garoua and Maroua to the Chadian border and N'Djamena. To Bangui: by road east from Douala through
Yaoundé and Bertoua to the border at Garoua-Boulaï. Rail or road for the first leg is chosen on
time, cost and what the cargo will stand.

### Documents
The bill of lading endorsed for transit, the commercial invoice and packing list, the cargo tracking
note the destination country requires, the transit declaration under the CEMAC inter-state transit
regime and its guarantee, and the waybill for each leg. At the border, the handover to the
destination customs.

### What you receive
A transit plan with the route and an honest lead time. A position at each checkpoint, not only at
the end. Discharge of the guarantee once the cargo is received at destination. Proof of delivery and
one invoice.

**Highlights.** Douala and Kribi to N'Djamena and Bangui · Rail to Ngaoundéré, road beyond · Transit declarations and guarantees · Exports out through Douala · Position at every checkpoint · Containers, break-bulk and relief cargo

**Meta title.** Road Transport in Cameroon | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Container haulage and general cargo by road from Douala and Kribi to Yaoundé, the regions and the borders, with loading checks and proof of delivery.

### What we do
We haul containers and loose cargo by road between the ports, your premises and your customers. We
match the vehicle to the load (container chassis, flatbed, curtainsider or low-loader), check the
cargo against the documents at loading, and return empty containers to the depot the carrier names
before the free days run out.

### Where
From Douala and Kribi to Yaoundé and every region of Cameroon. For cargo crossing into Chad or the
Central African Republic, see Hinterland Transit.

### Documents
The waybill for each truck, the delivery order and the container interchange at the port, the
packing list, and the delivery note signed at destination.

### What you receive
A loading report with photographs, the truck and driver's details, a position on the road, the
signed delivery note and the empty container's return receipt.

**Highlights.** Container haulage and general cargo · Douala and Kribi to every region · The vehicle matched to the load · Empty returns before the free days end · Signed delivery note

**Homepage line.** By wagon from Douala to Yaoundé and Ngaoundéré, where the line ends and the road begins.

**Closing line.** Rail where it pays, and we tell you plainly where it does not.

**Meta title.** Rail Freight in Cameroon | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Rail freight on Cameroon's line from Douala to Yaoundé and Ngaoundéré: wagon booking, loading at the terminals, and road onward to the north, Chad and the CAR.

### What we do
We book wagons, load your containers or bulk cargo at the terminal, move them by rail and unload at
the other end. Where the cargo goes further, the truck is waiting at the railhead.

### Where
Cameroon's railway runs from Douala to Yaoundé and on to Ngaoundéré, and stops there. It does not
reach Chad or the Central African Republic: cargo for Garoua, N'Djamena or beyond continues by road
from Ngaoundéré.

### Documents
The rail consignment note, the packing list, the delivery order from the port, and for cargo in
transit, the transit declaration that travels with it.

### What you receive
The wagon booking and the expected departure, a position at Yaoundé and at Ngaoundéré, the handover
to road, and proof of delivery. Rail pays on regular, heavy volumes; for a one-off light load we say
so and quote the road.

**Highlights.** Douala, Yaoundé, Ngaoundéré · Wagon booking and terminal loading · Road waiting at the railhead · Suited to regular, heavy volumes

**Meta title.** Warehousing in Douala | SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES

**Meta description.** Warehousing in Douala: receiving against the packing list, storage, stock reports and release on your instruction, with delivery by road across Cameroon.

### What we do
We receive your cargo, count it against the packing list, note any damage on the day, store it, and
release it on your written instruction. We stuff and strip containers, and deliver from the
warehouse by road.

### Where
Douala, close to the port.

### Documents
The packing list and arrival notice at receipt, the goods-received report, the stock statement, the
release instruction, and the delivery note for each dispatch.

### What you receive
A goods-received report on the day of arrival, with photographs of anything damaged. A stock
statement on the schedule you choose. Release within the time we agree, and the signed delivery note
for every dispatch.

**Highlights.** Receiving against the packing list · Damage noted on the day · Stock statements on your schedule · Container stuffing and stripping · Delivery by road
