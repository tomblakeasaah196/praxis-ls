# SMART LOGISTICS AND SERVICES: image prompts

For ChatGPT (or Gemini). Generate, pick the best of each, save with the file name given, and bring
them back in one folder. Everything here is uploaded through Settings › Website once PR 4 lands, so
any image can be replaced later without code.

## How to use

1. Open a new ChatGPT chat and paste the **House style** block first, on its own. It sets the look for
   the whole chat so every picture belongs to the same campaign.
2. Then paste one prompt at a time. Ask for 2 or 3 variations when the first is not right.
3. Ask for **landscape, 3:2** unless the prompt says otherwise. We crop on the site.
4. Reject any image with: readable text or numbers, a carrier's logo or colours (Maersk blue, MSC
   yellow, CMA CGM red and blue), warped cranes or ladders, extra fingers, faces looking at the camera,
   or that glossy "AI" sheen. Experienced shippers spot these at once.
5. Never use a generated person as a member of staff. Team and leadership portraits must be real
   photographs.

## House style (paste first)

```
For this whole conversation, create images in one consistent house style for a premium freight
forwarder that works between Central Africa and Europe.

Editorial documentary photography, the kind commissioned for an annual report: full-frame camera,
35mm or 50mm lens, natural light, honest and unstaged. True-to-life colour with slightly muted tones,
deep clean shadows, warm low sun or soft overcast light. Safety orange appears only where it occurs
naturally (hi-vis vests, lifting straps, a crane cab), never as a filter.

People are real-looking Central African and European professionals at work, wearing correct
protective equipment on ports and sites, with natural expressions, never looking at the camera, never
posing.

Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos, flags, brand colours or watermarks anywhere. Containers,
vessel hulls, trucks and aircraft are plain or weathered with no readable markings.

No illustration, no 3D render look, no HDR, no lens flare, no futuristic overlays, no holograms. Leave
calm negative space on one side of the frame for a headline. Confirm you understand, then wait for my
first prompt.
```

### MSC exception (put at the top of any prompt that shows MSC containers)

The owner approved MSC containers in the imagery (25 Nov 2026): the company works with MSC and the
real lettering makes a scene read as genuine. Zoom in on every result: a misspelled or warped "MSC"
looks more fake than no logo, so reject it.

```
Exception to the house style for this image only: containers may carry the real MSC shipping
line logo, spelled exactly "MSC", painted on the container sides as on a genuine box. No other
text, logos or readable markings anywhere.
```
